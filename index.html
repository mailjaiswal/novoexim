Pan-India Geo-Tagged Sourcing Map

We map every harvest to its certified Geographical Indication (GI) origin — from the Himalayan foothills and Gangetic sugarcane belts to Rajasthan's mineral beds.

GI Origin #1 · Rice · North India

Himalayan Basmati Belt

Sourced from Punjab, Haryana & Western UP. Glacial irrigation creates long-grain 1121 and Pusa Basmati renowned for aroma and 8.35 mm+ elongation.

GI Origin #2 · Jaggery · West & Central

Kolhapur Sugarcane Clusters

Chemical-free jaggery blocks and powder from Maharashtra & UP sugarcane belts, retaining natural sucrose and essential minerals.

GI Origin #3 · Multani Mitti · Rajasthan

Thar Mineral Clay Beds

High-purity Fuller's Earth / calcium bentonite clay extracted from Barmer & Bikaner deposits for cosmetic and industrial use.
