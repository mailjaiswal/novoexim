Privacy Policy

How Novo Exim collects, uses, and safeguards business contact and trade inquiry information on novoexim.com.

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 Entity: Novo Exim (Kammanahalli, Bengaluru, India) Contact: NovoExim2025@gmail.com

Novo Exim ("we", "our", or "us"), operated by proprietor Ankur Jaiswal and headquartered in Kammanahalli, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the business and personal information you share with us through https://novoexim.com/ and our direct trade communication channels.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse our website, submit a Request for Quotation (RFQ), or contact our trade desk, we may collect:

  • Business & Contact Details: Your name, company name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, and destination country/port.
  • Trade & Inquiry Details: Commodity preferences (such as Basmati Rice, Organic Jaggery, or Multani Mitti), order quantities, packaging specifications, and preferred Incoterms (FOB/CIF).
  • Technical & Server Logs: Standard non-identifying technical metadata (such as browser type, referring URL, and IP address) automatically processed by our static web hosting and CDN infrastructure for security and reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected strictly for legitimate B2B export and trade purposes:

  • Preparing formal price quotations, Proforma Invoices, and shipping estimates.
  • Coordinating product sample dispatches via international air courier for verified buyers.
  • Communicating regarding export documentation, quality certifications, customs clearance, and order fulfillment.
  • Complying with applicable Indian export regulations (including DGFT, GST, and customs requirements).

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business contact information to third parties. We may share relevant order details only when necessary with:

  • Freight forwarders, shipping lines, customs brokers, and courier services strictly to fulfill sample or container shipments.
  • Inspection agencies and regulatory authorities when required by applicable law or export compliance.
  • Infrastructure providers that host our static website (GitHub Pages) or deliver typography/form communications.

4. Data Retention & Security

We retain inquiry and trade correspondence only for as long as necessary to fulfill commercial discussions, ongoing export contracts, and statutory record-keeping obligations under Indian commercial and tax law. Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are maintained to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure.

5. Your Rights & Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your business contact details held by us at any time. To exercise these rights or opt out of follow-up trade communications, please email us at NovoExim2025@gmail.com.

6. External Links

Our website contains links to external platforms such as WhatsApp and LinkedIn. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party services.
