About Novoexim

Rooted in India, reaching the world.

Since 2008, Novoexim has been the trusted bridge between India's most conscientious producers and the world's most discerning buyers. We don't own farms. We don't run mills. What we do — and do with obsessive care — is curate.

From the basmati paddy fields of Punjab to the jaggery furnaces of Maharashtra and the mineral-rich soils of Rajasthan, our network of 200+ verified suppliers is selected for one thing: the quality they refuse to compromise on.

Hand-picked supply network

Every farm, mill and processor is personally audited — never a middleman.

Truly global logistics

Container, LCL and break-bulk from Nhava Sheva, Mundra and Chennai to 47+ ports.

Full documentation handled

COO, FSSAI, phytosanitary, fumigation, SGS and buyer-specific compliance.

Quality, before everything

If it doesn't pass our lab, it doesn't ship.

Quality is not a department at Novoexim — it's the lens through which every decision passes. Every container is backed by a paper trail of inspections, tests and certifications that your customs and quality teams can rely on.

Pre-shipment lab testing

Moisture, pesticide residue, heavy metals, microbiological — all tested at NABL-accredited labs before approval.

Full regulatory compliance

FSSAI licensed, APEDA registered, ISO 22000:2018, HACCP, and SGS third-party inspection on demand.

Traceable from farm to port
